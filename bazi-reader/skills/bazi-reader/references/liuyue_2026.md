## 2026 is 丙午年 — the themes of the year

- **Heavenly stem 丙 = yang fire** (七杀 for 庚金 people, 比肩 for 丙火 people, 偏财 for 壬水 people, etc.)
- **Earthly branch 午 = fire peak** (午 is where fire reaches帝旺 / emperor strength)
- **Broad 2026 theme:** heat, transformation pressure, high-stakes decisions, "refining fire" for metal day masters, "peak output" for fire day masters, "seasonal drain" for water day masters
- **Not ominous. Intense.** People with strong day masters thrive under 2026 energy. People with already-depleted systems should pace themselves.

## Month-by-month themes (for May–October, the most commonly-requested range)

### 癸巳 (May 6 – Jun 5) — "Water on Fire"
- 癸 (yin water) over 巳 (yang fire, + hidden 丙戊庚)
- **Top-line theme:** creative tension, rapid idea generation, "first sparks" energy. Ideas flow fast but feel unstable.
- **Favors:** brainstorming, pitching, launching drafts, starting projects, romantic sparks
- **Watch for:** over-commitment to brand-new ideas; restless nights; impulsive moves
- **Hidden 庚金 in 巳 is a long-life position for metal day masters** — if day master is 庚/辛, this is a re-birth month, not a struggle month
- **If liuyue 癸 stem is 伤官 to user's day master:** month amplifies their creative/unconventional side; encourage output; caution against speaking out of turn

### 甲午 (Jun 6 – Jul 6) — "Fire Peak"
- 甲 (yang wood) over 午 (yang fire peak)
- **Top-line theme:** maximum intensity, decisions feel amplified, don't be surprised by breakthroughs OR breakdowns
- **Favors:** closing momentum from earlier efforts, public-facing moments, competitions, visibility
- **Watch for:** impulsive decisions under pressure; burnout if you push without rest; conflicts flare
- **午午 self-punishment** when personal chart has 午: if the user has 午 in any pillar, note this month tests their patience with themselves
- **Broad advice:** this is not a month to start something new from cold. It's a month to execute what's already in motion, or to let the fire burn off what needed to go anyway.

### 乙未 (Jul 7 – Aug 6) — "Heat Settles"
- 乙 (yin wood) over 未 (yin earth, + hidden 丁乙己)
- **Top-line theme:** integration, the pressure softens, clarity returns after June's intensity
- **Favors:** strategic thinking, reviewing what worked, relationships, organizing resources
- **Watch for:** complacency; taking too long to act on clarified insight
- **午未合火:** continues the fire theme but starts channeling it into more stable containers

### 丙申 (Aug 7 – Sep 7) — "Pressure + Metal Strength"
- 丙 (yang fire) over 申 (yang metal, + hidden 庚壬戊)
- **Top-line theme:** peak-challenge-meets-peak-capacity. If the year asked a question, August forces the answer.
- **Favors:** closing major deals, public milestones, body at peak athletic output (for metal day masters)
- **Watch for:** the pressure is real — make sure you've recovered from June. Don't stack new big asks
- **For 庚金 day masters:** 申 = day master's禄地 (strength position). Body and mind at peak coherence. Best month of 2026 for high-stakes execution.

### 丁酉 (Sep 8 – Oct 7) — "Structure Settles"
- 丁 (yin fire) over 酉 (yin metal)
- **Top-line theme:** controlled refinement after August's blast furnace. What survived August now gets shaped.
- **Favors:** formalization (contracts, role definition, institutional commitments), polishing work, PR
- **Watch for:** over-polishing; perfectionism; slipping into bureaucracy
- **For 庚金 day masters:** 酉 is a personal power position (羊刃 or 禄 depending on chart). Strong month.

### 戊戌 (Oct 8 – Nov 6) — "Earth Takes Over"
- 戊 (yang earth) over 戌 (yang earth, + hidden 戊辛丁)
- **Top-line theme:** consolidation, land-on-the-ground. The fire of summer starts being stored, not burned.
- **Favors:** slow/deep work, foundation-building, hiring, long-term planning
- **Watch for:** inertia; "I'll deal with it later" avoidance; physical stiffness if not moving

## Do NOT do

- **Do not say "this month will cause X."** Use "the energetic tendency favors/disfavors X."
- **Do not predict specific events** — layoffs, illness, accidents, breakups, funding closes. This is general energy guidance, not divination.
- **Do not stack 20 ominous observations in a row.** Even for a truly intense month, include at least one framing that makes the intensity useful.
