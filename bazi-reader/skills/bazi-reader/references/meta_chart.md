# Meta × Personal Chart — Compatibility Reading

For the optional "you × Meta" section. Use this only when the user explicitly asks for it.

## Meta's charts (pick one based on context)

There's no single "correct" birth chart for a company. Pick based on what the user identifies with.

### Option 1: Facebook launch — Feb 4, 2004
- **Pillars:** 甲申 乙丑 戊寅 (time unknown)
- **Day master:** 戊土
- **Key note:** Feb 4, 2004 is literally the day of 立春 — Facebook was born on the solar-term boundary. This is unusually apt for a company that has always lived on identity boundaries (thefacebook → Facebook → Meta; social graph → VR → AI).
- **Fun angle:** Because the年柱 boundary fell on this exact day, the year pillar depends on the precise launch time. If launched before ~13:40 UTC, year is 癸未; after, it's 甲申. This ambiguity is... very Meta.
- **Use when:** the user identifies with "Facebook" / social layer / the legacy company

### Option 2: Meta rebrand — Oct 28, 2021
- **Pillars:** 辛丑 戊戌 丁巳 (time unknown; use approximate US afternoon of announcement)
- **Day master:** 丁火
- **Key note:** Young chart, strongly earth-influenced (丑戌). Identifies with the "Meta" branding specifically.
- **Use when:** the user joined post-2021 or identifies as a "Meta" employee culturally

### Option 3: IPO day — May 18, 2012
- **Pillars:** 壬辰 乙巳 庚辰 (time ~9:30 AM ET)
- **Day master:** 庚金
- **Key note:** IPO day is the "public entity" birth. Strong metal day master, earth support, wood-fire wealth structure. Historically compelling for modeling company performance.
- **Use when:** the user wants a "serious" business-oriented reading

### Default pick for Meta-internal post

Use **Option 1 (Feb 4, 2004)** with the ambiguity framed as a feature — it's a fun talking point and it's the broadest identification people have with the company.

## How to interpret the user × Meta dynamic

Compute the ten god of Meta's day master stem relative to the user's day master.

| User DM → Meta DM (Feb 2004 = 戊土) | Ten God | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 甲 / 乙 (wood) | Meta is 财 (wealth) | You generate resources from Meta; relationship is extractive-but-functional |
| 丙 / 丁 (fire) | Meta is 食神 / 伤官 (output) | You contribute energy to Meta; it's a natural output surface for you |
| 戊 / 己 (earth) | Meta is 比肩 / 劫财 (peer) | You and Meta speak the same native language; also: more internal politics |
| 庚 / 辛 (metal) | Meta is 印 (support / seal) | Meta supports and legitimizes you; also: risk of dependence on institutional validation |
| 壬 / 癸 (water) | Meta is 官 / 杀 (authority) | Meta is a controlling structure for you; relationship is formative-but-tense |

## Tone for the Meta section

- Treat Meta as a person, not a god. "Your relationship with Meta," not "Meta's power over you"
- Acknowledge the ambiguity of using a company chart — frame it as "one way to read it" rather than "the truth"
- If the dynamic is tense (官/杀 relationships), don't be alarmist — frame as "natural dynamic that works if both sides grow"
- Do NOT use this to predict the user's specific career outcome at Meta

## Sample language

> Your 甲木 day master relates to Meta's 戊土 as **偏财** — Meta is "wealth" to you. That doesn't mean they pay you a lot (they do, but that's not the point). It means the relationship is transactional and pragmatic: you extract what you need, they extract what they need, and when the extraction stops working for either side, it's time to move on. The 甲木 person doesn't root deeply in 戊土 soil — you grow, take what you need, and at some point you outgrow the pot.
