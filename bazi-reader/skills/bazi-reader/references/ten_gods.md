# Ten Gods (十神) — Modern Interpretations

Each of the 10 gods describes how another stem/branch relates to the day master. Keep interpretations playful, concrete, and modern — no mysticism or absolute statements.

## Self-group (同我)

### 比肩 (Bi Jian) — "Peer / Shoulder"
- Same element, same polarity as day master
- **Energy:** peers, siblings, friendly competitors, founder community, teammates at your level
- **When strong:** independent, self-reliant, community-oriented
- **When overdone:** stubborn, refuses help, splits focus with too many equals around
- **Modern frame:** your LinkedIn network, your cohort, your YC batch

### 劫财 (Jie Cai) — "Rob Wealth"
- Same element, opposite polarity
- **Energy:** siblings or peers who compete with you for resources; the friend who one-ups you
- **When strong:** socially dynamic, reads the room, fierce when defending
- **When overdone:** resource leakage, rivalry burns you out, partnerships go sideways
- **Modern frame:** the coworker gunning for your promo, the co-founder conflict, the friend who copies your ideas

## Output-group (我生)

### 食神 (Shi Shen) — "Eating God"
- Element you generate, same polarity
- **Energy:** steady creative output, enjoying your craft, the hobby that pays
- **When strong:** prolific, expressive, natural communicator, health-positive
- **When overdone:** hedonistic, loses discipline, scatters in pleasant activities
- **Modern frame:** your writing practice, your Notion templates, that side project you actually enjoy

### 伤官 (Shang Guan) — "Hurting Officer"
- Element you generate, opposite polarity
- **Energy:** unconventional brilliance, rule-breaking creativity, "I'll show them" drive
- **When strong:** genius-level output in your field, disruptive innovation, talented rebellion
- **When overdone:** burns bridges with authority, legal/HR issues, isolates from institutions
- **Modern frame:** the 10x engineer who won't go to meetings, the founder who roasts incumbents on Twitter, the polymath who's always right and always in trouble

## Wealth-group (我克)

### 偏财 (Pian Cai) — "Biased Wealth"
- Element you control, same polarity
- **Energy:** windfall money, deal-making, multiple income streams, sexual chemistry
- **When strong:** opportunities find you, comfortable around money, attractive to others
- **When overdone:** volatile fortunes, distractions from core work, commitment issues
- **Modern frame:** equity windfalls, your side hustle that went viral, the person you can't stop thinking about

### 正财 (Zheng Cai) — "Proper Wealth"
- Element you control, opposite polarity
- **Energy:** stable income, accumulated assets, the spouse who's good with money
- **When strong:** reliable resources, builds wealth steadily, committed partner
- **When overdone:** risk-averse to a fault, materially focused, stuck in the "good enough" job
- **Modern frame:** your salary, your 401k, your long-term relationship

## Authority-group (克我)

### 七杀 (Qi Sha) — "Seven Killings"
- Element that controls you, same polarity
- **Energy:** raw external pressure, powerful opponents, high-stakes challenges that forge you
- **When strong (and day master can handle it):** you handle crisis better than anyone, leadership under fire, Olympic-level performance
- **When overdone / day master weak:** breakdown, injury, hostile takeover, burnout
- **Modern frame:** the VC who challenges your entire thesis, the competitor who's coming for your market, the deadline that forces your best work

### 正官 (Zheng Guan) — "Proper Officer"
- Element that controls you, opposite polarity
- **Energy:** structured authority, institutional recognition, controlled challenge
- **When strong:** respected by organizations, natural team lead, good with hierarchy
- **When overdone:** over-identifies with institution, loses personal edge, becomes the bureaucrat
- **Modern frame:** your manager, your board, the formal review process, the industry award

## Support-group (生我)

### 偏印 (Pian Yin) — "Biased Seal"
- Element that generates you, same polarity
- **Energy:** unconventional support, mentor from nowhere, sudden insight, intuition spike
- **When strong:** learns fast from unusual sources, spiritual insight, makes connections others miss
- **When overdone:** scattered focus, chases too many teachers, overthinks
- **Modern frame:** that one ex-Google mentor, the book that changed your life, the 3am idea that actually worked

### 正印 (Zheng Yin) — "Proper Seal"
- Element that generates you, opposite polarity
- **Energy:** steady mentorship, formal education, institutional backing, mother-figure support
- **When strong:** respected credentials, loving support system, academic grounding
- **When overdone:** stuck in student mode, can't leave the nest, over-reliance on authority's approval
- **Modern frame:** your degree, your long-time advisor, the foundation the family built for you

---

## Interpretation rules

1. **Ten Gods are NOT moral labels.** 七杀 doesn't mean "bad" — it means "intense authority figures." 伤官 doesn't mean "broken" — it means "unconventional output."
2. **Context matters more than the label.** 七杀 with strong day master = leadership under pressure. 七杀 with weak day master = stress breakdown risk. Same character, opposite outcomes.
3. **Absence matters too.** A chart with no 印 may need to ask for help more. A chart with no 财 might underprice themselves. Don't only read what's there.
4. **Flow matters most.** Look at which ten gods are generating/supporting which. A chart where 伤官 → 财 (creativity generates wealth) is a different life from one where 伤官 → 官 (creativity attacks authority).
